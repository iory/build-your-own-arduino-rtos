## On the board

```{figure} ../_static/ch06_fault.gif
:name: fig-ch06-fault
:width: 100%

`p` lists the tasks and `d` triggers a division by zero. After the fault `Heartbeat` keeps going, and only `Crash_Div` becomes `TERMINATED`.
```

## In the simulator

Without the board, the same firmware running in the {doc}`simulator <../getting-started/simulator>` looks like this.

```{figure} ../_static/sim_ch06_fault.gif
:name: fig-ch06-sim
:width: 80%

Sending `d` makes the `Crash_Div` task fault on a division by zero, and `FAULT DETECTED` with the cause is printed. L (Heartbeat) and the `[Heartbeat]` lines keep going. (Recorded in the simulator.)
```
