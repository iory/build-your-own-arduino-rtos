## In the simulator

Without the board, the same firmware running in the {doc}`simulator <../getting-started/simulator>` looks like this.

```{figure} ../_static/sim_ch04_blink.gif
:name: fig-ch04-sim
:width: 80%

L (LED1) toggles every 100 ms and the left half of the matrix (LED2) every 500 ms; the right half flips each time the busy `Heavy` task finishes a round. While `Heavy` keeps the CPU busy, L and the left half keep their rhythm. (Recorded in the simulator.)
```
