```{admonition} Column: what is a servo horn?
:class: tip

A servo horn (or just horn) is the disc- or arm-shaped part fitted onto the servo's
output shaft. It passes the servo's rotation on to the part it drives, such as a leg.
The tip of the output shaft has fine teeth (a spline), and the inside of the horn's hole
has the same teeth. The teeth mesh and a screw holds them together, so the force is
transmitted without slipping.

The mesh can only be shifted one tooth at a time, so the direction in which you fit the
horn decides "which way the leg points when the servo is at its centre angle".
```
